Faradine Systems , a leader in developing rugged and reconfigurable hardware solutions, announced today that it has been selected by AFWERX for a $1.25 million SBIR Phase II contract to develop and produce a smart signaling device designed to further enhance the U.S. Air Force's tactical communications capabilities in partnership with goTenna.
