Christopher Boyd brings extensive product development and off-grid communications technology experience to goTenna. JERSEY CITY, NJ (November 16, 2023) — goTenna, the world’s leading mobile mesh networking company, today announced the appointment of Christopher Boyd as VP of Product. Boyd brings over twenty years of experience in hardware and software product-oriented roles such as Product Manager, Solutions Architect, and Technical Marketer.
