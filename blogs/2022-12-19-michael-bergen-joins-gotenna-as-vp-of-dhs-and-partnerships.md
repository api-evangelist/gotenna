Bergen brings extensive national security and public sector experience to goTenna Monday, Dec 19th, 2022 ( JERSEY CITY, NJ ) — goTenna, the company behind the world’s most advanced, infrastructure-free, and scalable mobile mesh networks, today announced the appointment of Michael Bergen as VP of DHS and Partnerships. Mr. Bergen brings 20 years of leadership experience in bringing commercial technology to mission-centric customers.
